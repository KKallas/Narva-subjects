## Prototyping: Milestone 1 — System Specification

**Workload:** 10 hours | **Assessment:** 20 points | **Teamwork:** teams of 4

### What you do

The team picks one of three projects and writes a system specification detailed enough that another team could build the system from it.

**A: Syringe Dispenser** — a dispensing system mounted on a Dobot MG400 for applying silicone to a solenoid valve cap. Continues the work from semester 1. The specification must choose between three actuation options (pneumatics, servo motor, the robot's own axis) and justify the choice.

**B: Sumo Robot** — a SMARS-inspired battery-powered robot with an Android phone as the brain and an ESP32 MCU interfacing the motors. Built for Narva College's summer robot arena event (5v5 format, 3×3 m arena). The summer deadline is real — your schedule has to account for it.

**C: MG400 Modular Workbench** — three subprojects: (1) a modular worktable for mounting 3D-printed structures, (2) a transport case for the whole kit, (3) an electronics tool kit (USB-C powered where possible). The goal is a portable workstation a student can rent and take home.

### What the specification must contain

System overview, functional requirements (at least 10, MoSCoW prioritised), performance targets with measurement methods, component selection with justifications, mechanical concept, electrical architecture, software architecture, test plan and development schedule.

### Feasibility analysis

Three analyses in Jupyter notebooks: a physical feasibility check (critical performance parameter), a cost-performance trade-off analysis, and a review of prior work (A) or a technology survey (B and C).

### Required for the defence

**Only a link to the git repository.** The repo must contain:
- All documentation in `.md` format
- Software files (Fusion 360, draw.io, etc.) uploaded as an archive
- A GitHub/GitLab milestone for each of the following milestones (2–5)
